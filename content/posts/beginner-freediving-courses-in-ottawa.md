If you’ve found this page, it means that you’re likely looking for a simple, local way to get into freediving. It’s a tough sport to enter at times, especially in Canada where the cold temperatures and lack of visibility and diverse sea life don’t make the idea of “holding your breath underwater” very appealing.

All of that changes now. While I’m in Canada this season, I’m hosting two, beginner level freediving courses, that are appropriate for people with lots of swimming experience, or with very little. People that have tried diving before, and complete newcomers to the concept. The course is designed to teach you all of the skills and safety measures to ensure that you and your diving buddies can have fun, safe experiences in the water together.

Our Molchanovs Wave 1 course dates this season are: <br>September 12 & 13th<br>September 19 & 20th

Both two-day courses include theory lectures, dry skills training, 3 open water sessions, and a separate pool session that will be held during our club nights in September or October, and can be scheduled based on your availability.

The cost of the course is $550 all-in, and includes gear rentals. Our wetsuits are 5mm thick, two-piece, open-cell freediving wetsuits in a variety of sizes from TruDive, featuring Yamamoto 45 neoprene, one of the leading materials for wetsuits in the industry. In addition, we have Molchanovs fins, masks & snorkels and neoprene socks and gloves to stay as warm as possible in the water.

The open water sessions will be held at Morrison’s Quarry, just south of Wakefield, QC and about 30 minutes of driving from downtown Ottawa.
