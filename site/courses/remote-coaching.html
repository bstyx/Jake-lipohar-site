Remote Coaching

Progress while we're apart.

Book a callSend me a message

Overview

Continue your progression whether we are in the same city or across the globe. Jake builds customized workouts for you and checks in on video calls, with the work targeted at your weakest areas. Let's raise the floor so your baseline is better than before.

What's included

  • Approx. 2 video calls per month
  • Customized dry, pool, depth, or gym workouts
  • Work targeted at your weakest areas

Getting started

Message Jake to get set up. Remote coaching is billed monthly.

Frequently asked questions

I want to learn without a crowd. Do you offer private courses and coaching?

Absolutely. Get in touch to discuss pricing for private courses and training sessions.

My friends and I are in a different city and want to try freediving, will you come visit?

Get in touch to discuss the possibility of having us come out to visit you, either in Canada or elsewhere abroad. We are excited about freediving and will happily travel to meet you there!

When do you announce courses in Ottawa or abroad?

Course dates are typically announced on the @jake.freedives and @FreediveOttawa Instagram accounts. You can also subscribe to the email newsletter.
